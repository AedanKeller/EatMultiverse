## Episode VIII: The Solar System (photoreal 3-D)
Everything you see here is real data, rendered in 3-D with three.js. NASA material is public domain (US government works). NASA does not endorse this game.
- **NASA 3D Resources** (public domain). https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources
  Models: Hubble, Chandra, Spitzer, Swift, CloudSat, Landsat 7, Aura, Galileo, Voyager, Cassini-Huygens, Dawn, Solar Dynamics Observatory, Parker Solar Probe, Deep Impact, Space Shuttle, Saturn V, Skylab, the ISS, the Apollo Lunar Module, an astronaut, a lost glove, a pistol-grip tool, CubeSats and asteroid Bennu (1999 RQ36). I decompressed, simplified and re-packed them with gltf-transform.
  Planet and moon maps: Mars, Phobos, Deimos, Venus, Jupiter, Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Saturn, Titan, Enceladus, Neptune, Pluto and Charon, plus the Tycho star map used as the sky.
- **Earth:** NASA Blue Marble "Land Shallow Topo" (public domain), via Wikimedia Commons.
- **The Moon:** NASA Scientific Visualization Studio CGI Moon Kit, LRO color map (public domain). https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720
- **Moon rocks** (used as asteroids) by **Poly Haven**, CC0. https://polyhaven.com/models
- The Sun, Uranus, Saturn's rings and the black hole are made in code.
